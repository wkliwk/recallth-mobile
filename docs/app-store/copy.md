## Description (4000 chars max)

```
Recallth is your personal AI health advisor — with memory.

Tell it once about your body, supplements, health goals, and conditions. From then on, every health question gets a personalised answer grounded in your specific stack, without re-explaining your context each time.

SUPPLEMENT CABINET
Store your full supplement, vitamin, and medication list. Add names, doses, frequency, and timing. Reorder by drag-and-drop. Pause items temporarily without deleting them. Get instant interaction alerts when you add something new — with severity ratings (minor / moderate / major) so you know what to watch for.

AI HEALTH ASSISTANT
Chat with an AI advisor trained on supplement and nutrition research. Ask about interactions, optimal timing, what to combine, and why. The AI references your actual cabinet — "given your 5g daily creatine and current magnesium dose…" — so answers are never generic. Conversations persist so you can pick up where you left off.

DAILY DOSE TRACKING
See your full supplement schedule organised by morning, midday, evening, and night. Tap to log each dose as you take it. Log late doses with a single tap. Undo mistakes instantly. Smart reminders name your specific supplements so you always know what's due — and a missed-dose nudge fires 2 hours later if you forget.

STREAK & BADGES
Build momentum with daily adherence streaks. Earn achievement badges at 7, 14, 30, and 90-day milestones. Missed a day? Use the 24-hour grace window to recover your streak by logging yesterday's doses.

HEALTH PROFILE
Structured storage for the context that makes AI answers meaningful: body stats, conditions, allergies, lifestyle (diet, exercise, sleep), and active health goals. Update any field at any time — the AI picks up the change immediately.

HISTORY TIMELINE
Everything in one place: past AI conversations, cabinet changes, profile updates, and dose logs — in chronological order. Tap any conversation to re-open it. Filter by type to find what you need.

TRENDS & INSIGHTS
Track dose adherence over time with weekly charts. Log daily mood and energy check-ins. View AI-generated supplement interaction summaries and monthly adherence reports.

Recallth is not a medical app. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting or changing any supplement or medication regimen.
```
